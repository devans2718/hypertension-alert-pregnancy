# App assets

`logo.svg` and `logo.png` (512 × 512) are the team's logo, also the Discord server's icon: a three-segment ring in the colour-blind-safe Okabe–Ito blue, yellow and vermilion, around a gauge.

The gauge at the centre is the "gauge" outline icon from [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) (MIT licence, © 2020–2026 Paweł Kuna; notice below), drawn inline as SVG paths. The PNG is rendered from the SVG.
